My role

Four jobs, one robot.

Aug 2024 — present · 18+ hours a week in the STEM lab

Builder

Mechanical design, structural assemblies, prototyping and the failure analysis that follows.

Strategist

Reading the meta, choosing what our robot should be good at, and what it can afford to be bad at.

Drive team

Driving matches, adapting mid-match, and turning what I felt on the controller into build notes.

Notebook

Documenting decisions, tests and dead ends — the record that won us the Create Award.

Doing all four is the reason this portfolio isn't a list of results. When you drive the robot you built, and then write down why it lost, you stop being able to blame anyone else's part of the system. Every failure traces back to a decision, and most decisions were mine to make.
